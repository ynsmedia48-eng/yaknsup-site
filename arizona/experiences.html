Guided Canyon Tour

Our signature experience. A knowledgeable guide leads your group through the dramatic canyon passages of Canyon Lake — narrow slots, towering walls, and scenery most visitors never find on their own. Perfect for all skill levels.

  • Daily 10:30 AM – 1 PM (2.5 hours)
  • Small groups — intimate and unhurried
  • All gear included — PFD, paddle, briefing
  • No experience necessary

Full Moon Paddle

Paddling under a full moon is one of those experiences that's genuinely hard to describe until you've done it. The canyon walls glow, the water is glass, and everything feels a little magical. Glow gear provided. Zero experience needed.

  • Monthly on the full moon
  • 5:30 PM – 9:30 PM
  • Glow gear and lights included
  • Absolute beginners welcome
