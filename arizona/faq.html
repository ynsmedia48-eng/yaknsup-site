General
No experience needed — zero. Every rental starts with a short safety and paddling briefing from one of our crew. Most people feel comfortable within 5–10 minutes on the water. We genuinely want you to have a great time, not struggle.
Paddling is very safe when you follow basic guidelines. Every participant wears a properly fitted life jacket (PFD) on the water — no exceptions. We provide a briefing covering safety rules, what to do if you fall in, and how to paddle efficiently. Our guides are trained in water safety and first aid.
We welcome all ages. Kids can ride along in a tandem kayak with an adult. There's no minimum age, but children must be able to wear a properly fitting PFD and follow basic safety instructions. For very young kids, we recommend a tandem kayak rather than a paddleboard.
It happens! Wear clothes you're okay getting wet. Your life jacket keeps you afloat and we'll cover re-entry technique in the briefing. Canyon Lake is generally calm — falling in is more of a funny story than a big deal.
Rentals
Every rental includes: the watercraft (kayak or paddleboard), an adjustable paddle, a properly fitted personal flotation device (PFD/life jacket), and a pre-paddle safety briefing. You do not need to bring any gear — just yourself, sunscreen, and water.
Single Kayak & SUP: $35 (2hr), $65 (4hr), $75 (full day). Tandem Kayak: $50 (2hr), $90 (4hr), $110 (full day). PDL Fishing Kayak: $150 (2–6hr). Standard Fishing Kayak: $80 (2–6hr). Use code YNSCASH for a cash discount. See full pricing →
Yes! We accept cash and credit/debit cards. If you're paying with cash, use promo code YNSCASH at checkout for a discount. Exact change appreciated but not required.
Single kayaks are sit-on-top, stable, and great for solo paddlers. Tandem kayaks fit two people — great for pairs or a parent + child. PDL fishing kayaks have a pedal drive so your hands stay free for fishing. Standard fishing kayaks are paddle-powered with rod holders and gear storage.
Yes — we provide the kayak and gear, but a valid Arizona fishing license is your responsibility. You can purchase one online through Arizona Game & Fish before your trip.
Booking & Policies
We strongly recommend booking online in advance, especially on weekends and holidays. Walk-ups are welcome when we have availability, but we can't always guarantee a spot. Booking online also saves time at check-in.
Full refund for cancellations 24+ hours in advance. Within 24 hours — we'll reschedule you for a future date (no cash refund). No-shows are non-refundable. Cancellations due to weather conditions we call are always fully refunded.
Safety first. If we determine conditions aren't safe for paddling (lightning, high winds, flooding), we'll contact you and issue a full refund or reschedule. If weather is cloudy but safe, we operate as normal — bring a layer just in case.
Yes — all participants must sign a liability waiver. You can sign online in advance through our booking system (recommended), or at check-in. Minors require a parent or guardian signature.
Locations
16802 AZ-88, Tortilla Flat, AZ 85190 — about 45 minutes east of Phoenix via US-60 and AZ-88 (Apache Trail). It's a scenic drive through the Superstition Mountains. Download directions before you leave — cell service drops near the lake.
Cell service is notoriously spotty at Canyon Lake. Text us at (480) 420-8144 and the message will go through when you get signal. Better yet — book online in advance and arrive 15 minutes early. We'll be there ready for you.
Yes — Canyon Lake Marina has on-site parking. Cell service is spotty on the way out, so download directions in advance and book online before you leave.

Groups
We recommend reaching out at least 2–3 weeks in advance for groups of 20 or more, and 1 week for smaller groups. Popular dates (weekends, holidays, summer) book up fast. The earlier the better.
