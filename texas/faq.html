General
No experience needed — zero. Every rental starts with a short safety and paddling briefing from one of our crew. Most people feel comfortable within 5–10 minutes on the water. We genuinely want you to have a great time, not struggle.
Paddling is very safe when you follow basic guidelines. Every participant wears a properly fitted life jacket (PFD) on the water — no exceptions. We provide a briefing covering safety rules, what to do if you fall in, and how to paddle efficiently. Our guides are trained in water safety and first aid.
We welcome all ages. Kids can ride along in a tandem kayak with an adult. There's no minimum age, but children must be able to wear a properly fitting PFD and follow basic safety instructions. For very young kids, we recommend a tandem kayak rather than a paddleboard.
It happens! Wear clothes you're okay getting wet. Your life jacket keeps you afloat and we'll cover re-entry technique in the briefing. Lake Lewisville is generally calm — falling in is more of a funny story than a big deal.
Rentals
Every rental includes: the watercraft (kayak or paddleboard), an adjustable paddle, a properly fitted personal flotation device (PFD/life jacket), and a pre-paddle safety briefing. You do not need to bring any gear — just yourself, sunscreen, and water.
Pricing is handled through our FareHarbor booking system — click Book Now to see current rates for kayaks, paddleboards, and specialty rentals. Use code YNSCASH for a cash discount. See all rentals →
Yes! We accept cash and credit/debit cards. If you're paying with cash, use promo code YNSCASH at checkout for a discount. Exact change appreciated but not required.
Single kayaks are sit-on-top, stable, and great for solo paddlers. Tandem kayaks fit two people — great for pairs or a parent + child. Fishing kayaks have rod holders and storage for your gear. SUP paddleboards offer a great full-body workout with wide-open views.
Yes — we provide the kayak and gear, but a valid Texas fishing license is your responsibility. You can purchase one online through Texas Parks & Wildlife before your trip.
Booking & Policies
We strongly recommend booking online in advance, especially on weekends and holidays. Walk-ups are welcome when we have availability, but we can't always guarantee a spot. Booking online also saves time at check-in.
Full refund for cancellations 24+ hours in advance. Within 24 hours — we'll reschedule you for a future date (no cash refund). No-shows are non-refundable. Cancellations due to weather conditions we call are always fully refunded.
Safety first. If we determine conditions aren't safe for paddling (lightning, high winds, severe weather), we'll contact you and issue a full refund or reschedule. If weather is cloudy but safe, we operate as normal — bring a layer just in case.
Yes — all participants must sign a liability waiver. You can sign online in advance through our booking system (recommended), or at check-in. Minors require a parent or guardian signature.
Location
20400 Hackberry Creek Park Rd #200, Frisco, TX 75036 — located at Hidden Cove Park on Lake Lewisville. Easy to reach from Frisco, The Colony, Denton, and all of the DFW area. Download directions before you leave — GPS can be tricky through the park entrance.
Yes — Hidden Cove Park charges a separate $20/vehicle entry fee at the gate. This is a park fee paid to the park, not to Yak N Sup. Plan ahead and have cash or card ready at the entrance.
Yes — Hidden Cove Park has on-site parking near the marina. The $20/vehicle park fee covers your parking. Book online before you leave and plan to arrive 15 minutes early.
Thu: 10am–8pm · Fri & Sat: 10am–9pm · Sun & Mon: 10am–8pm · Closed Tue & Wed. Hours may vary seasonally — check our booking page for current availability.

Season Passes
Texas season passes are valid at Hidden Cove Marina, Lake Lewisville, TX only. Have questions about your pass? Contact us and we'll sort it out.
Lake Lewisville is great year-round, though peak season is spring through fall. Your pass is valid for the full season from purchase date. Check with us for current seasonal hours.
Absolutely. Purchase a pass and we'll send a gift certificate the recipient can redeem. Or check out our gift cards which can be used toward any purchase including passes.
Groups
We recommend reaching out at least 2–3 weeks in advance for groups of 20 or more, and 1 week for smaller groups. Popular dates (weekends, holidays, summer) book up fast. The earlier the better.
Our group packages are designed for 10 or more people. For smaller groups, you can book regular rentals directly through our booking system — no group package needed.
Yes — we work with corporate groups regularly and offer customized pricing for team-building events. Fill out our group inquiry form and mention it's a corporate event for a custom quote.
