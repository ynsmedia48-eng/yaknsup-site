Who We Do This For

Every Kind of Group

We've done it all. Here's what we're great at:

Bachelorette & Bachelor

The ultimate day on the water before the big day. Paddling, laughing, and making memories on Lake Lewisville. We'll make sure the group of honor has the best time.

Corporate & Team Building

Get your DFW team off their screens and onto the water. Nothing builds camaraderie like paddling together. We handle the logistics — you take the credit.

Birthday Celebrations

Make this birthday unforgettable. Group paddle, good vibes, and the birthday person rides free during their birthday week. We'll customize the experience for you.

School & Youth Groups

Safe, fun, and educational. Our guides are trained to work with youth groups — from elementary school field trips to high school adventures and summer camps.

Church & Community

A great way to bring your community together outside the walls. We love working with faith-based and civic groups looking for something different on Lake Lewisville.

Reunions & Celebrations

Family reunion, class reunion, retirement party — any reason to celebrate on the water is a good one. We'll make it easy and memorable at Hidden Cove.

Process

How Group Booking Works

Simple from start to finish.

1

Submit Inquiry

Fill out the form below with your group size, date, and location. We'll get back to you within 24 hours.

2

We Plan It

Your coordinator builds out the experience — route, timing, equipment, and any add-ons you want.

3

Confirm & Book

Review the plan, lock in your date with a deposit, and send the booking link to your group.

4

Show Up & Paddle

Arrive, get a briefing, grab your gear, and have the best time. We handle everything else.
