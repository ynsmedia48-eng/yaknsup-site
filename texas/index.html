Good to Know

Before You Head Out.

Quick answers about paddling Lake Lewisville.

Zero. Every rental starts with a short safety and paddling briefing. Most people feel comfortable and ready within a few minutes on the water.
Single Kayak & SUP: $35 (2hr) / $65 (4hr) / $75 (full day). Tandem Kayak: $50 / $90 / $110. PDL Fishing Kayak: $150. Standard Fishing Kayak: $80. Paying cash? Use code YNSCASH for a discount.
We're open Thursday, Sunday, and Monday from 10 AM – 8 PM, and Friday and Saturday from 10 AM – 9 PM. We're closed Tuesday and Wednesday.
Yes — there's a $20 park entry fee collected by LCRA at the gate. This is a park fee, not a rental fee. It goes to the park, not us — but it's worth every penny once you see the lake.
Cancellations made 24 hours or more before your reservation receive a full refund. Same-day cancellations may be subject to a fee. Contact us and we'll work with you.
Kids 8 and up can paddle tandem with an adult. All minors must wear a properly fitted life jacket, which we provide. Safety is always our first priority.
Absolutely — just no glass containers on the water. Pack snacks, water, and non-alcoholic beverages in a soft cooler or dry bag. Staying hydrated out there is important.
